AïA Evaluation Framework

When young people talk to AI, how do we know whether it's helping them or could be causing harm? We answer that question by measuring what AI systems do.

Mission

AIA is a way to test how AI interacts with young people. Experts identify what to look for, and we create the open source infrastructure to measure how the AI behaves, for example, whether it encourages someone to think for themselves or makes decisions for them.

Goals

The results help companies improve their AI and help policymakers set rules. We identify what experts across cultures agree on, what may need to be different in different places, and what needs more research. As researchers learn more about the long-term effects, we update the guidance.

The goal is to help AI support young people as they grow.

Unique Methodology

A neuroscience lead technology

The CALIBER methodology assesses a range of parasocial behaviors to ensure that when a child or teen has a conversation with an AI system, we can measure whether the conversation could lead to an unhealthy relationship between the child and the AI chatbot. These measurements are designed to help conversational AI application builders to design safer applications for children and teens.
