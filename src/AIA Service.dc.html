Available AI safety tools miss how children actually form trust and attachment with conversational AI. AïA Safety Builder will help AI systems stakeholders not only check for harmful content but also identify behavioral risks.

Go beyond content moderation

Most AI safety tools focus only on screening harmful content. AïA Safety Builder also evaluates AI powered responses for child developmental behavioral risk.

AïA Safety Builder helps AI companies by:

Checking for harmful and explicit content

Harmful, violent and explicit content has no place in the discourse AI applications and models have with children and teens. Our tool stimulates models to verify they never cross this line.

Ensuring AI systems enhance child development

Even with appropriate content, AI systems could isolate children and teens from their world. AïA Safety Builder helps application and model builders evaluate the tradeoff between product engagement and the risk of harming their younger customers.
