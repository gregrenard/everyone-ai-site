Who We Are

everyone.AI is an international, multidisciplinary team bringing together experts in developmental neuroscience, artificial intelligence, education, and public policy.

Our team, contributors, and advisors combine scientific expertise, technical knowledge, and diverse perspectives to advance Beneficial AI for Children. We believe meaningful progress requires collaboration across disciplines, generations, and sectors, including the active participation of young people.

Our Values

Our work is grounded in the core principles of ethical AI. AI must be safe, protecting children from AI-related risks; ethical, respecting their rights and well-being; fair, actively addressing discriminatory bias; inclusive, offering equitable access to all; transparent, with clear and understandable processes; and privacy-respecting, protecting children’s data.

At everyone.AI, we bring an additional perspective by placing children’s cognitive, social, and emotional development at the heart of our approach. Children are not simply younger adults: their needs and capacities evolve from early childhood through adolescence and into young adulthood. AI systems must be designed, evaluated, and governed with these differences in mind.
