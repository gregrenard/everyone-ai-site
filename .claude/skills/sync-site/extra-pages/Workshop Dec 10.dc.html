An event co-organized with Consulate General of France in San Francisco

An official partner event of the Paris AI Action Summit

​In partnership with the Consulate General of France in San Francisco and as an official partner event of the Paris AI Action Summit, we are launching an International Coalition dedicated to addressing the challenges of safeguarding children's development in the AI Era.

​This coalition aims to bring together the most relevant stakeholders among governments, AI companies, product developers, NGOs, AI and child development experts, to promote responsible and beneficial AI for future generations.

​We are thrilled to announce our inaugural production workshop, which will take place in San Francisco on December 10th.One of the expected output is for all the engaged leading parties to write a set of actions and recommendations that they would be willing to adhere to and promote at the AI Action Summit, in the view of the latest research, advancements in artificial intelligence, and anticipated challenges related to AI safety for children.

​We invite all interested parties to apply to join our coalition.
