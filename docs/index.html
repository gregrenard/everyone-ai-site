Our Mission

501(c)(3) Nonprofit Organization

Our mission is to anticipate, educate, and evaluate the risks and opportunities of Artificial Intelligence for children, adolescents, and young adults (0–25).

Grounded in research from developmental neuroscience, psychology, science of learning, and AI, we move from evidence to practical action, to ensure that AI supports healthy cognitive, social, and emotional development for the next generation.

Our Vision: A future where Beneficial AI supports the healthy development of every child, everywhere.

Our Actions

Applied Science

The Scientific Foundation of our Three Pillars

iRAISE
International Coalition

Bringing together researchers, industry leaders, governments, international organizations, and civil society to advance Beneficial AI for Children. Through scientific collaboration, policy dialogue, and youth participation, iRAISE translates research into actionable recommendations, standards, and best practices.

Education

Translating neuroscience and AI research into practical knowledge through interactive workshops, training sessions, conferences, and educational resources. Our programs help educators, families, companies, and young people understand AI's opportunities and challenges, with a focus on learning, critical thinking, and well-being.

AïA
Evaluation Framework

Advancing independent, science-based evaluation of how AI systems interact with children and young people. Through AïA Safety Builder, we assess developmental risk signals and provide measurable indicators to help AI developers and decision-makers improve AI safety and accountability.

Why Now?

The rapid evolution of AI technologies is outpacing the development of child-specific safeguards. As AI's adoption accelerates, waiting to course-correct could have irreversible impacts.

It's imperative to anticipate potential pitfalls and proactively establish guidelines for our young users, ensuring their safety and well-being in the digital age.
