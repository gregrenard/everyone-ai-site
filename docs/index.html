Our Mission

Our mission is to anticipate, educate, and evaluate the risks and opportunities of Artificial Intelligence for children, adolescents, and young adults (0–25).

Grounded in research from developmental neuroscience, psychology, science of learning, and AI, we move from evidence to practical action, to ensure that AI supports healthy cognitive, social, and emotional development for the next generation.

Our Vision: A future where Beneficial AI supports the healthy development of every child, everywhere.

Our Actions

Applied Science

Feeds our three pillars

iRAISE
International Coalition

Bringing together researchers, industry leaders, governments, educators, and civil society to advance responsible AI and promote child-centered policies, standards, and best practices.

Education

Providing training, workshops, conferences, and resources that help companies, educators, parents, and young people navigate the opportunities and challenges of AI.

AïA
Evaluation Framework

Enabling AI developers and decision makers to make AI systems safer for children, teenagers and young adults.

Our latest publication:

Mapping of GenAI impacts on child development

This report examines how generative AI use may shape children's cognitive, emotional, and developmental trajectories across four age bands, from birth to age 18. Drawing on developmental science, neuroscience, and emerging evidence on youth AI use, it translates converging scientific insights into developmentally grounded governance priorities for G7 policymakers.

Read More

Why Now?

The rapid evolution of AI technologies is outpacing the development of child-specific safeguards. As AI's adoption accelerates, waiting to course-correct could have irreversible impacts.

It's imperative to anticipate potential pitfalls and proactively establish guidelines for our young users, ensuring their safety and well-being in the digital age.
