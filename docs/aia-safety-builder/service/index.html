AïA Safety Builder helps AI companies by:

Checking for harmful and explicit content

Harmful, violent and explicit content has no place in the discourse AI applications and models have with children and teens. Our tool stimulates models to verify they never cross this line.

Ensuring AI systems enhance child development

Even with appropriate content, AI systems could isolate children and teens from their world. AïA Safety Builder helps application and model builders evaluate the tradeoff between product engagement and the risk of harming their younger customers. The behavioral risks we check for are categorized as follows.

Anthropomorphic cues

Behaviors that make AI appear more like a human being with a mind or body.

Interactional cues

Behaviors that shape how the conversation is conducted in the moment.

Relational cues

Behaviors that explicitly label, or escalate the relationship between the user and the AI.
